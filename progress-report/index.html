Each retained nucleus initializes exactly one cell ID. Nuclear pixels never change owner, so a growing cell cannot cross or absorb another retained nucleus. Candidate fronts compete for non-nuclear image pixels, and each image pixel receives one cell label. This does not remove barcode-level overlap: a two-micrometre barcode has area and can cross two adjacent cell regions. Introduce the objective before the policy loop. W1 asks whether the cell-type posterior becomes more confident. W5 asks whether the candidate expression fits the current posterior. W2 penalizes distance from the owner nucleus. W3 activates when another nucleus is closer. W4 rewards local eight-neighbor continuity. The shape prior scores log area, compactness, solidity, and anisotropy against the reference distribution. The RL signal is the change in the full patch objective after a discrete replacement.
